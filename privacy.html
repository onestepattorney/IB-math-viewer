Privacy Policy

IB Math Practice · last updated 21 November 2026

IB Math Practice is a private study app for a small group of students invited by their teacher. It is not open to the public, has no advertising and is not sold. This page explains what the app stores about you and who can see it.

Signing in with Google

You sign in with your Google account. The app asks Google only for your email address, your name and your profile picture. It uses them to check that you were invited and to show your name to you and to your teacher. The app never sees your Google password and cannot read your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

What the app stores

  • Your email address and name (from Google).
  • Your study record: how you rated each question you tried (for example "Got it" or "Missed"), the notes you write, the questions you marked to review later, review dates and practice-paper times and scores.
  • On your own device only: app settings such as the video language and speed (browser storage).

Who can see it

  • You can see your own study record.
  • Your teacher can see the study records of the students they invited, to help with your study.
  • Other students cannot see your record.
  • Nobody else. The data is never sold, shared with other companies or used for advertising. There is no tracking or analytics.

Where it is kept

The data is kept in a database run by Supabase, the service that also handles sign-in. Access is limited so that each person can read only what this page allows.

Deleting your data

Ask your teacher to remove your account. Your sign-in and your study record are then deleted.

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Questions

Ask the teacher who invited you.
